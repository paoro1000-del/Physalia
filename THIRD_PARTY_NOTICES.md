## Japanese name components

`physalia/japanese_names.py` contains Japanese surname and given-name components derived from the `japanese-personal-name-dataset` project by shuheilocale. The surname candidates are its first 1,000 entries by estimated population. The given-name candidates use its curated male and female popular-name subsets and spelling variants. These are not an official, nationwide top-1,000 ranking of given names.

Source: <https://github.com/shuheilocale/japanese-personal-name-dataset>

The source project declares this dataset under the MIT License. It describes surname population estimates as based on Myoji-Yurai Net and given-name spellings as cross-checked and extended using Wikidata (CC0) and the National Diet Library Web NDL Authorities. The source data was accessed on 2026-11-04.
